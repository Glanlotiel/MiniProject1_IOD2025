The world of Oxweth

This is my in progress world of Oxweth. I am currently writing the setting alongside finding time to work on the map inbetween projects. Oxweth itself is a high magic setting. A continent recovering from the scars of recent war. Droccolan, to the north, waged an agressive war against the key shipping bottleneck: Haelor ruled by the country Perthinon. This war, dubbed "The War of Kings" raged for twelve years which ripped a large portion of magic from the region among other horrors. The party gathering in this world arrives a year after peace finds its way to Oxweth to adventure in the land...

Why make the site?

I am a dungeon master, and putting my resources in one spot where I can easily access things makes my life easier. Most dice rollers I have used in the past also lack the "make character" button that I put into the main roller and that makes my site a more unique one.

Future Plans

I ideally would like to extend the site to host a library of character sheets stored internally designed by my self. I would also like to implement animations for the dice so it's not just spewing numbers at the user, style the dice picker and buttons around the site. It is already responsive on Mobile, but I need to keep in mind mobile users for input into character sheets.
